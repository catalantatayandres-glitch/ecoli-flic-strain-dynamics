# Results — Community Analyses

Key figures from the community-level analysis of all 96 DADA2 parameter combinations.
All analyses were performed on mock, individual, and negative control samples jointly,
after rarefaction to 1 500 reads per sample.

---

## ASV_sharing_all_params.png

![ASV sharing](ASV_sharing_all_params.png)

ASVs classified as shared across all levels of a parameter (green) or unique to one
specific level (red), for each of the five DADA2 parameters independently.

**Key findings:**
- A clear hierarchy of parameter influence emerges across all five parameters
- **Pooling strategy** dominates: 90.1% of ASVs are unique to the `TRUE` pooling level — full pooling drives the detection of a large number of sequences not recovered under any other strategy
- **Filter mode** shows a strong effect: 67% of ASVs are unique to the `Loose` setting, indicating that relaxing quality thresholds substantially inflates the detected ASV set
- **Omega** has a moderate-to-strong effect: 26.9% of ASVs are unique to the least stringent threshold (1e-40)
- **Error model** shows only moderate influence: 80.5% of ASVs are shared across Binned and PacBio, with PacBio producing 19.5% unique ASVs
- **Band size** has no detectable influence: 100% of ASVs are shared across both levels

> Note: a high proportion of unique ASVs at a given level does not by itself confirm whether those sequences represent true biological signal or noise — this requires the additional analyses below.

## PERMANOVA_results.png

![PERMANOVA results](PERMANOVA_results.png)

R² values (proportion of variance explained) for Aitchison (red) and Jaccard (blue)
distances, with point size proportional to the F-statistic and p-values from 999
permutations shown on the right.

**Key findings:**
- **Sample type** is the strongest driver of community structure (R²= 0.115 Aitchison, R²= 0.076 Jaccard, both p = 0.001), confirming that true biological differences dominate over any pipeline-driven effects
- **Pooling strategy** is the only pipeline parameter with a strong and significant effect under both distance metrics
- Critically, the pooling effect is **much stronger under Jaccard** (R²= 0.084) than under Aitchison (R²= 0.015) — this discrepancy is informative: Jaccard is based on presence/absence while Aitchison is abundance-weighted. The fact that pooling strongly structures which ASVs are *detected* but barely affects their *relative abundances* raises the possibility that the extra sequences introduced by TRUE pooling are low-abundance and may not represent genuine biological variants
- **Filter mode** reaches significance only under Jaccard (R²= 0.009, p = 0.014), consistent with the same pattern: Loose filtering inflates the detected ASV set without meaningfully shifting abundances
- **Omega, error model, and band size** show no significant effect under either distance metric

## PCoA_Aitchison_6panels.png & PCoA_Jaccard_6panels.png

![PCoA Aitchison](PCoA_Aitchison_6panels.png)
![PCoA Jaccard](PCoA_Jaccard_6panels.png)

PCoA ordination of all 96 × samples coloured by each of the six variables
(sample type, pooling, filter mode, Omega, band size, error model) for Aitchison
distance (top) and Jaccard distance (bottom).

**Key findings:**
- Under **Aitchison distance** (PC1 = 14.6%, PC2 = 8.3%), individuals, positive controls, and negative controls separate clearly — confirming that biology, not pipeline choice, is the primary driver of community structure
- The **TRUE pooling** samples form a distinct trajectory along PC1 across all three sample types. This means the pipeline-driven signal under full pooling is strong enough to partially override biological differences between sample types — consistent with the possibility that TRUE pooling introduces sequences shared across samples regardless of their true biological composition
- Under **Jaccard distance** (PC1 = 0.6%, PC2 = 0.6%), the low variance explained on both axes means visual patterns should be interpreted cautiously. Nevertheless, TRUE pooling samples form a clearly distinct cluster, consistent with the high R² observed in PERMANOVA — TRUE pooling produces maximally dissimilar communities at the presence/absence level
- Taken together, the stronger pooling effect under Jaccard than Aitchison across both PERMANOVA and PCoA consistently points to the same conclusion: **the extra ASVs introduced by TRUE pooling are predominantly low-abundance, raising the possibility that these sequences represent noise rather than true biological variants**
- **Omega, band size, and error model** produce no visible clustering in either ordination, fully consistent with their non-significant PERMANOVA results
